# Revised 5e Encounter module, version 1.2.0

This is a separate, bundled V2 module. It is not discovered by the legacy V1 module loader. The host selects it only when the GM explicitly pins `example.revised5e` on the default-off V2 API. Its exact lock digest covers the manifest, rules/extension source and declarative combat contribution.

The encounter contains two original player characters, Mira Vale and Oren Pike, and an original enemy, the Mire Warden. Their names, descriptions and stat choices are original example content. Club and shortsword weapon dice are SRD 5.2.1 equipment data.

## Included rules

- Each combatant rolls one host supplied d20 for initiative. The initiative modifier is the Dexterity modifier. Highest total acts first and the order repeats each round. Initiative ties use the encounter's stable actor order as a documented table procedure; the SRD leaves tie ordering to the GM and players.
- A turn permits one action: Attack or Disengage. Movement can be taken before the action; this focused example preserves its existing table procedure of advancing initiative immediately after Attack, so post-Attack movement is deferred. End Turn allows a creature to pass without taking an action and advances initiative, including after Disengage. The host accepts actions only for the current actor and rejects reused, defeated, stale or out-of-turn actions. Players can act only for their enrolled character; only the GM can act for the enemy.
- The attack total is a host supplied d20 plus the weapon's selected ability modifier, plus proficiency bonus only when the actor is proficient. A natural 1 misses and a natural 20 hits and is a critical hit, regardless of AC. Otherwise the total must meet or exceed target AC.
- On a hit, roll the weapon die and add the same ability modifier used for the attack, with damage floored at zero. On a critical hit, roll that weapon die twice and add the modifier once. Damage reduces current HP to a minimum of zero; zero HP marks the encounter participant defeated and ends this example encounter.
- Movement uses a module-owned square grid: each cell is 5 feet and combatants occupy one cell. A movement command supplies at most eight adjacent orthogonal steps; each costs 5 feet and cannot exceed remaining Speed. Occupied and blocked cells cannot be entered. The grid and token scale are example-specific. Diagonals are explicitly rejected; difficult terrain, squeezing, larger creatures, forced movement, teleportation and other special movement are not implemented.
- Reach for this example uses the SRD square-grid distance rule at 5 feet per cell (Chebyshev distance, including diagonals for range measurement), with each combatant's configured melee reach. The only implemented opportunity-attack exception is the Disengage action. It consumes the actor's action for the turn and suppresses opportunity attacks against that actor for the remainder of that turn. Forced movement and teleportation are not approximated as ordinary movement; they are unsupported.
- When the mover's submitted path first leaves one eligible hostile creature's reach, the command suspends before committing any movement effect. The durable continuation records the path and pre-exit square; the mover remains at its original persisted position while pending. The GM is the responder for the example enemy. On an attack choice, the host rolls one d20 and, only on a hit, weapon damage; the enemy's reaction is consumed even on a miss. The attack is resolved immediately before the mover leaves reach. If the mover is reduced to 0 HP, movement stops at the pre-exit square and only movement through earlier submitted segments is spent; otherwise the full path resumes. Decline/expiry resumes the path without consuming the enemy's reaction.
- End Turn allows a creature to pass without taking another action, making the action-costing Disengage usable in the encounter. The responder's reaction is replenished at the start of that creature's next turn.
- Mira is a module-defined level-1 Fighter with two Second Wind uses. On her turn, Second Wind is a Bonus Action: it spends one use, rolls one host-supplied d10, adds Fighter level (1), and restores that many Hit Points up to her maximum. It is independent of the example's Attack/Disengage action, and only one Bonus Action can be spent per turn. Using the feature at full HP is legal, consumes the use and Bonus Action, and has zero healing after the maximum-HP cap. Other example combatants use a `not-modeled` class resource profile.
- A GM can complete a Short Rest only after the encounter is complete, with no durable reaction continuation pending. This example control is the GM's attestation that one uninterrupted hour of downtime has elapsed (the SRD's duration and activity limits); the host does not simulate the passage of time. Each living Fighter in the encounter regains exactly one expended Second Wind use, capped at its maximum. It does not restore HP, spend Hit Point Dice, or reset other resources. A Fighter at 0 HP is ineligible because a creature must have at least 1 HP to start the rest. Hit Point Dice recovery is explicitly deferred.
- This increment supports one opportunity-attack trigger per movement command and one GM-controlled responder. Multiple potential responders, repeated crossings, simultaneous reactions and player-controlled creature reactions are explicitly unsupported. The example has no difficult terrain, conditions, cover, resistance, extra attacks, spellcasting, other healing features, Hit Point Dice spending or Long Rest recovery. Movement commands are atomic: no prefix segment is separately committed before the reaction; the final continuation commit includes the attack result and movement result in one host transaction.

Mechanics references in the [official SRD 5.2.1 PDF](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.2/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf): attack rolls, natural 1/20 and AC (pp. 6–7); Bonus Actions (p. 10); initiative, turns, movement and the one-action allowance (pp. 12–13); grid distance, opportunity attacks, Disengage, reaction timing and reach (pp. 12–14); damage, HP and critical damage dice (pp. 16–17); Short Rest duration, activity and benefits (p. 186); Fighter level-1 features, two Second Wind uses, healing and one-use Short Rest recovery (pp. 46–47); weapon proficiency and weapon dice (pp. 89–90). The encounter's stable-order tie procedure, five-foot orthogonal-only map, GM-controlled enemy reactions, one-responder limit, end-at-zero encounter behavior, GM attestation of an uninterrupted one-hour rest in a completed encounter, and atomic whole-path continuation are example-specific table/implementation procedures, not claims about SRD requirements.
